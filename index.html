Our anchor floor brings together Dr D Geoffreys, Dr A Jenkin, and Dr Jess Stanbridge alongside Ubuntu Wellness and Peter Jones — creating a full-spectrum primary care and wellness environment.

Medical bookings include digitised intake forms completed before arrival, reducing waiting room time and giving your doctor full context on day one.

  • GP & specialist consultations — Drs Geoffreys, Jenkin & Stanbridge
  • Ubuntu Wellness — holistic and integrative health
  • Digital patient intake forms pre-consultation
  • Medical aid billing assistance
  • In-building referral network across all four floors

Third Floor

Therapy & Wellness Suites

The third floor is home to Rochelle, Trudy & Pam, Michelle, Yvonne & Wilscha, Linda Brash, and Hiatus Cape Town — offering private therapy, counselling, and restorative wellness in a discreet, calm environment.

Client notes never touch the central system. Practitioners sync their own calendars and manage their own client relationships in full privacy.

  • Individual & group therapy sessions
  • Hiatus Cape Town — restorative wellness & retreat services
  • Linda Brash — specialist therapy practice
  • Recurring appointment scheduling with calendar sync
  • Full client anonymity — private notes stay with the therapist
